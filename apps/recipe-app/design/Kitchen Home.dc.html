The loop
Home lists every recipe with search and tag filters. Tapping a row opens its page.
Add recipe opens the builder. Save writes the recipe into the list, flags it New, and lands you on its page.
Recipe page is the one you approved — tabs, servings stepper, heart, mark as cooked. Back returns to the list.
Hearts and cooked marks persist per recipe while you move around.
Desktop below shares the same state: click a row there and the phone follows.
