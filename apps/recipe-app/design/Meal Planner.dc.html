Assumptions I made

From your answers: the five screens you picked, fully clickable, phone-first with one desktop layout, macros at a balanced weight. The week runs three strength days, three light cardio days and one rest day (Mon 31 Aug – Sun 6 Sep; today is Wednesday). Recipes and the plan are invented — send real ones and I'll swap them in. Shopping is in the frame too since the brief asks for it; say the word and I'll cut it.

Design reasoning

The week is a ruled ledger, not a card feed: day rules do the organising so a glance finds today. Numbers sit in a fixed second cell under each day header, always in the same place, muted — a target you can ignore. Red appears three times only: today, the primary action, a done check. Every interaction is one tap deep.
