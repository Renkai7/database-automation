<objective>
Build the second harness this phase needs and prove the migration history is consistent with it:
a `test:history` suite that runs against genuinely empty `@testcontainers/postgresql` instances,
a committed machine-readable record of its result with a cheap assertion in the default suite,
and the three proofs it exists for — full history against an empty database, newest migration
against an existing one, and a migration tampered after classification still refused at
execution.

Purpose: RUN-05, RUN-06 and criterion 1. Testcontainers gives "genuinely empty" by construction
with a dynamic port, which the pinned development container cannot; and a runner bug that fails
to refuse destroys a throwaway container instead of the developer's database.
Output: `vitest.history.config.ts`, `scripts/history-status.ts`, `scripts/history-suite.ts`,
`docs/migration-history-status.json`, and three integration tests under `tests/history/`.
</objective>
