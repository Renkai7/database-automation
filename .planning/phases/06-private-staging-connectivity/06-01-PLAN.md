<objective>
Prove, end to end and by hand, that a private path to a real staging PostgreSQL exists on the
owner's actual Coolify instance — then write down what was actually observed and close the open
decision D4 against that evidence rather than against community reports.

Purpose: this is the phase's riskiest architectural assumption. `permitopen` names one literal,
sshd-resolvable destination and performs no name lookup or pattern matching; if this Coolify
instance offers no stable such destination, every later plan in this phase changes shape. Proving
it first, on one thin slice, is what keeps that discovery cheap.

Output: a committed, argument-free staging connectivity probe; the staging half of the connection
pin; a performed recon record that closes `docs/00-current-state.md` sections 4 and 6; and D4
resolved in `docs/decisions.md`.
</objective>
